## English

Subtitle: `Voice chat for Mumble servers`

Promotional text:

> Join your Mumble community with voice chat, messages and photo sharing. Connect directly, find local servers, or return to a favourite.

Description:

> Connect to Mumble servers from your iPhone for conversations with friends,
> gaming groups and communities.
>
> - Talk using voice activation, push-to-talk or continuous transmission.
> - Connect directly, browse public servers and discover servers on your local network.
> - Keep favourite servers and quickly return to recent connections.
> - Send text and photos to a channel or another user.
> - Manage certificate identities and server access tokens.
> - Use a native iOS interface with support for 12 languages.
>
> Requires a compatible Mumble server with Opus support and an internet or local
> network connection. Server operators set their own access rules and policies.
> Microphone permission is needed to speak; local-network permission is needed
> for LAN discovery and connections.
>
> This client is based on the open-source Mumble iOS project. blakebill
> publishes and supports this build. It is not an official release from the
> Mumble project.

The independent-build statement assumes publication from the fork owner's
account. Update it only if the upstream project has authorized a different
relationship. Do not claim end-to-end encryption, perfect audio, or completed
moderation/account-deletion features.

Keywords: `mumble,voice,chat,voip,push to talk,channels,gaming,community`
